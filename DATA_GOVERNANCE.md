# ACE Data Governance & Student Privacy Protection

## 1. Compliance
- Built to align with FERPA (student academic privacy) and global data protection standards (GDPR / Indian DPDP Act 2023).
- Strict user consent required before candidate profiles are visible in the Recruiter Talent Radar.

## 2. Right to Data Portability
- Students can export their entire digital academic and co-curricular footprint in standardized JSON format (`/student/passport`).
