# ACE AI Governance, Transparency & Fair Evaluation

## 1. AI Evaluation Ethics
- All AI interview evaluations provide explicit, transparent rubric breakdowns (Technical, Innovation, Presentation, Viability).
- AI evaluations NEVER automatically disqualify students from recruiter pipelines; AI serves as a coaching and diagnostic aid.

## 2. Plagiarism & Anti-Cheat Protocols
- Code submissions in hackathons and Project Lab undergo automated AST similarity analysis.
- Unattributed copy-pasting of open-source boilerplate is flagged for human judge review.
