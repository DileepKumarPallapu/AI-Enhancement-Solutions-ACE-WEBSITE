# AI Ecosystem Specification: 6 Core Modules

## 1. AI Recommendation Engine (`/for-you`)
- **Scoring Pipeline**: Evaluates student department fit, skill alignment, geographic proximity, and time urgency.
- **Match Output**: Produces a contextual match percentage (e.g., `98% Match for You`) and explanation.

## 2. AI Smart Search (`/search`)
- **Natural Language Parsing**: Analyzes unstructured student queries (e.g., *"Free hackathons in Chennai under ₹500"*) and decomposes them into explicit filter parameters.

## 3. ACE AI Assistant ("Ask Zuzu")
- **Grounded Conversational Intelligence**: Floating drawer grounded in loaded event payloads to answer eligibility and registration queries with zero hallucination.

## 4. AI Event Verification & Quality Scanner
- **0–100 Quality Score**: Audits listings for poster clarity, physical address legitimacy, ticket clarity, and duplicate detection prior to publishing.

## 5. AI Content Studio
- **Organizer Copywriting**: Generates SEO descriptions, structured FAQs, social copy, and OCR metadata from uploaded posters.

## 6. AI Engagement Enhancement
- **Deadline Triggers**: Sends proactive notifications 48h before registration deadlines.
